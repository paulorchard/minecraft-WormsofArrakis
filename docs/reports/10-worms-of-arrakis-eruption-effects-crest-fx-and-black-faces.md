Status: partly — all three parts built (the crest redone to the revised Part 2 with the tail), 193 unit tests pass (0 failures) and two dedicated-server smoke starts ran clean; nothing was seen in a client (hard rule), so the look of the eruption, the crest effects and the tail, and whether the light fix cures the black faces, are unverified. ## What was built

**Part 1 — the eruption (fx-4).**
- `fx/EruptionFx.java`: the owner's twelve rows as data (names, numbers exactly as in the prompt) and the constants `ERUPTION_SUSTAIN=5.0`, `ERUPTION_JETS=14`, `JET_REROLL=0.6`, jet angle sigma 0.12, strength 0.5..1.4, shade 0.85..1.15, fade 15 % in / 40 % out.
- `FxPlayer`: the particle code now works on "lanes" (a row at a site). Eruption rows are lanes anchored at the breach; bursts are spread evenly over their `over` seconds; sustain rows run to breach + 5 s. Jets are re-rolled every 0.6 s (`FxPlayer.jetsFor(seed, epoch)`, deterministic); a jets particle picks one of the 14 jets, takes its angle plus a gaussian of 0.12 rad and multiplies up and out by the jet's strength. Placements ring, disc, jets, curtain and the velocity rule are as written. The eruption uses its own random stream, so the older rows come out exactly as before.
- New key `eruption=on|off` (default on) on crest, emerge, crash, engulf, generated into `moves`; spec field p31. `fxcount` scales the eruption rows like the others.
- `FxSink.Spawn` carries endScale, alpha, shade, drag and a `shaped` flag (eruption rows only). New particle type `CHUNK`.
- `fx/PuffTracker.java` (pure): follows every puff, once per client tick sets size = start x (1 + (endScale-1) f) and opacity = row alpha x fade(f), drops it at death. `FxClient` ticks it, also when no worm is left.
- `FxClient` sink: colour = tint x shade (clamped to 1), the sheet's gravity on the puffs, drift factor with the row's own drag.
- Budget raised from 12000 to 14000; the first half of an eruption burst is never thinned (see answer 1).
- `fx` chat/log show the eruption rows (`eruption: ...` lines, `fx sheet:` lines); the `crash fx:` log line says `eruption=on|off`.

**Part 2 — the crest (fx-5, tail-1), as revised.**
- Crest effects on by default (`fx=off` still works). Crest height default 0.4 -> **0.7** (`Moves.DEFAULT_CREST_HEIGHT`; the owner's chosen default).
- `fx/HeadTop.java` (new, pure): the mandible shells of the mouth are built as the renderer builds them (`HeadMouth.mandibles`, rolled so the upper jaw is on top) and moved with the frame of the head ring; the highest vertex (or the rim of the head ring) is the highest point of the head, and the centre of the head points within 0.6 blocks of the base level is the head contact point.
- `FxAnchors` for a crest: breach = the instant the highest point of the head reaches the base level; approach = max(0, breach - 4.0); apex = tip highest; closeStart/riseEnd from prompt 08 (riseEnd = apex + close_end); fallStart = apex; reentry = first instant after the apex that the tip is below; end = the last instant the last joint is above the base level + 0.3 (not the end of the cycle). Crash, emerge, engulf unchanged. The anchors are cached per move (a crest takes 0.05 s at W16 to 0.3 s at W48 to compute).
- Crossings (`FxGeometry.at(m, t, mouthDeg)`): up to two. Tip above the sand -> the first crossing from the tip is the entry; tip below -> the first is the exit and a second, if the body still passes it, is the entry; before the apex, while the axis has no crossing but the head already touches the sand, the entry is the head contact point. Crash, emerge, engulf: one crossing as before.
- Crest sound sheet as data (`fx/CrestFx.java`): the owner's 19 rows (the bone creak stays in the data but is off), the two whole-event rumble rows have computed repeat counts (`floor((end - start) / every) + 1`) and use one constant event id (`CrestFx.RUMBLE_EVENT`, a one-line change when the owner picks the earthquake sound). Sounds play at the entry hole except the rows from the reentry, which play at the exit hole.
- Crest particle rows: tremor grains overhead from the approach to the breach; the breach rows and the whole eruption at the entry; the rise rows (pouring, dust puffs) at the entry from breach + 0.2 until the end; mouth clamp breath at riseEnd; no hold rows; "Fall: pouring" at the exit from the reentry to the end; the re-entry rows at the exit; the base mask rows (dust skirt, low cloud, sand skirt) at every crossing that exists from the breach to the end; the **exit eruption**: the twelve eruption rows again at the exit from the reentry at 40 % of the counts and rates, the sustain rows at most 3 s (`EruptionFx.EXIT_FACTOR/EXIT_SUSTAIN`), with jets of their own; key `xeruption=on|off` (default on, crest only). A crossing that does not exist emits nothing.
- **Tail (tail-1, crest only)**: `geometry/TailMath.java` (pure). Keys `tail=on|off`, `taillen` 0.1..0.6 (0.3), `tailnub` 0.03..0.4 (0.12), `tailcurve` 0.5..2.5 (1.0), clamps noted in chat. r(k) = r0 up to n0 = round(n x (1 - taillen)), then r0 x (nub + (1 - nub)(1 - u)^curve); each segment from n0 on is a frustum of max(8, round(pi W r(k)/r0)) outer faces, same texture and per-face colour jitter as the body, closed at the last joint by a rounded cap (three bands and a pole). `WormSkin.build` stops the shingle skin at joint n0 and adds the tail; the renderer draws it in its own `submitCustomGeometry` and runs the quad check on it. Tail off gives the old skin byte for byte (tested); crash, emerge, engulf have no tail. Spec fields p33 xeruption, p34 tail, p35 taillen, p36 tailnub, p37 tailcurve (appended only when not default).

**Part 3 — the black faces (light-1).**

- Commands: `light`, `light dump`, `light log on|off|all`, `light force full|auto|<sky> <block>`.

**2. Particle calls per type.**
- Size: `Particle.scale(sheet size / real width)` once at creation for all types (grain 0.15 or 0.10, chunk 0.25/0.15 = 1.67x, dust_plume sizes 3.2..7 via 0.225, cloud 3.6 via 0.5625). Shrink: the puffs' `quadSize` field is written each tick (field made public by the access transformer; no bounding-box work, no accumulated ratio error). Alpha: the `alpha` field is written each tick (also made public: **`setAlpha` could not be made public**, `SpellParticle` overrides it as protected and the game recompile refuses that), alpha x fade(f). Colour: public `setColor(r,g,b)` = tint x shade, clamped to 1. Gravity: the `gravity` field (AT) set for the puffs; `DustPlume` multiplies it by 0.88 per tick so its -0.01 fades. Drift: the velocity is multiplied by `ParticleModel.driftFactor(type, life, drag)` with the row's own drag.
- Nothing the API refused. Block grains and chunks are opaque and keep vanilla's gravity 1.0 (equal to the previs's 1) and friction 0.98 (the chunk's 0.99 is compensated by the drift factor). What the owner sees instead of nothing: the cloud's own first-1/32 grow-in on top of my fade-in (a very small effect).
- Cost of the shrink/fade bookkeeping (pure part, JIT warm, from `numbersForTheReport`): 10 us per tick at 2000 puffs, 30 us at 6000, 59 us at 12000 (the game's two field writes are included, the rendering is not). The peak followed at W16 is about 6000 puffs, so about 30 us per tick. The client logs `fx puffs: N followed now, peak P, worst tick U us` every 5 s while a worm is on.

**3. The chunk type and the block-display experiment.** Built: `PType.CHUNK` = a sand block particle (`BlockParticleOption`, sand) scaled 0.25/0.15, long life, high speed, spread 0.2-0.3, vanilla gravity and block collision; a flat billboard, not a tumbling cube. ## Surprises, problems, deviations from the prompt

- **The prompt changed under me.** I first built Part 2 to the earlier text (crest anchors from the tip, 76 sounds, the rise rows until the apex, no tail) and committed it with Part 1 (`6fd0c61`). The revised Part 2 replaced it: new anchors from the head, new crossings, a sound sheet of its own, the exit eruption, the height default 0.7, the tail. `fx-5` was therefore moved (a local tag, nothing is pushed) to the commit of the revised crest; `fx-4` stays on 6fd0c61; `tail-1` is on the same commit as `fx-5` (the crest effects and the tail share spec fields and files). Three jars were deployed in all (after the eruption, after the black faces, after the revised crest).
- **Head contact of the mandibles is approximate.** The breach is measured with the mouth open at the default 45 degrees and without stagger (the stagger turns the jaw by less than one face, under a block); a move spawned with `open_deg` or `mouth=closed` still has its crest breach at the 45 degree value. It matches the owner's previs table within 0.05 s.
- **`approach` is 0.00 for every crest I tried** (the breach comes at 1.6-3.4 s, earlier than the 4 s lead), so the tremor grains and sounds start at once.
- **Access transformer needed.** `setAlpha`, `quadSize` and `Particle.gravity` are protected in 26.1.2 and there is no public alpha setter. I added `META-INF/accesstransformer.cfg` for the three fields (first attempt with `setAlpha` broke the recompile of the game sources because `SpellParticle` overrides it as protected). This also fixes prompt 09's "gravity cannot be set"; it is applied to the eruption rows only, the prompt 09 rows are unchanged. Building now needs `JAVA_HOME` set to temurin-25 (the default JDK of this machine cannot recompile the game sources after an AT change).
- **Budget trips at W16** (answer 1).
- **The default crest body is shorter than the arc** (80 segments at W16): the body has left the entry hole before the exit effects start, so entry and exit eruptions are not at once for that length (a 300-segment body overlaps them); the test uses the long body for the "any" rows.
- **Tail faces at the seam**: the first tail ring has round(pi W) faces and the last shingle ring floor(pi W) (the same at W16, one more at some widths), so a seam of at most one face can show at ring n0; the taper itself is outer faces only, no liner.
- **Crest hole/entry reference points** are placed beside the body, not on its axis, to satisfy "never inside the body".
- **H4 was a dead end** for the built meshes; the listener still reports it live.
- **No `chunks=display` key**, the experiment says no.
- Smoke limitation as before: a dedicated server with no player loads no worm chunk, so the client features (puffs, light samples) are not exercised there.
